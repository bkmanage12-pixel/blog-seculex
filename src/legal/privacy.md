# Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: April 2026*

Your privacy is critically important to us. At SecuLex, we have a few fundamental principles:

1. **Minimal Data Collection:** We do not ask for personal information unless we truly need it.
2. **No Data Selling:** We do not share your personal information with anyone except to comply with the law or protect our rights.

## Website Visitors

Like most scholarly websites, SecuLex collects non-personally-identifying information of the sort that web browsers and servers typically make available, such as the browser type, language preference, and the date and time of each visitor request.

## Gathering of Personally-Identifying Information

We only collect personally-identifying information (like email addresses) if you choose to contact us or subscribe to a newsletter.

If you have questions about deleting or correcting your personal data, please contact the site administrator.
