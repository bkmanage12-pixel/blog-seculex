# Statement of Academic Integrity

SecuLex is committed to maintaining the highest standards of intellectual honesty and scholarly rigor. All analyses, commentaries, and research published on this platform adhere strict protocols regarding original authorship and citations.

## Plagiarism Policy

Zero tolerance is maintained for plagiarism. All references, quotes, and paraphrased concepts must be thoroughly cited using widely accepted academic citation formats (e.g., Bluebook or OSCOLA).

## Peer Review and Editorial Standards

While this blog operates as independent commentary, content is subject to internal editorial review to ensure doctrinal accuracy, objective legal analysis, and logical integrity. 

*Any errors of fact or law will be publicly corrected with a dated addendum appended to the original publication.*
